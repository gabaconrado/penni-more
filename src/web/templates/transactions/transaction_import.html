Bulk import transactions

Upload a CSV to validate and preview every row before importing. Previewing never creates transactions.

CSV requirements

  • UTF-8, no larger than 1 MiB, with at most 1,000 nonblank data rows.
  • type is exactly expense, income, or transfer in lowercase.
  • account_id is the CSV account ID shown on account details and must identify an account you own. For a transfer, it is the source and must be a bank account.
  • target_account_id is required for transfers and identifies a distinct bank or card account you can access in the same currency. Leave it blank for expenses and income.
  • date uses exactly YYYY-MM-DD. amount is positive, has exactly two decimal places, and contains no sign or currency symbol.
  • category matches an active admin-managed category name without regard to capitalization.
  • additional_data is blank or a flat JSON object of text keys and text values; arrays, nested objects, and non-text values are not accepted.

Required header, in this exact order:

type,name,description,category,date,amount,account_id,target_account_id,additional_data
